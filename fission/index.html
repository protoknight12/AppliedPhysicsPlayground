    How to run it, and what the model does

    Operating. With automatic rod control on, the rods chase the power setpoint and (if linked) the turbine follows. Turn it off and drive the rods yourself. Boron is the slow, even knob in water reactors. Watch xenon after a power cut: it keeps building for hours and can pin a reactor shut (the "iodine pit").

    The plant view is live and clickable: start or stop the primary, feedwater and circulating-water pumps, trip or reset the turbine, open the generator breaker, change the tower fans or drop the grid.

    Start-up. Press Cold shutdown, then withdraw rods (or set auto with a small setpoint). After criticality the power climbs on a stable period; at a few percent reset the turbine from the plant view, let it reach 3000 rpm and synchronise, then raise the load.

    Physics. Point kinetics with six delayed-neutron groups (implicit, so even fast spectra and prompt-critical excursions are stable). k is built from the four-factor formula — η, ε, resonance escape p, thermal utilisation f — plus leakage from core size, reflector and migration area, blended into a fast-spectrum balance when moderation is weak. Doppler, moderator-temperature and void coefficients are not set by hand: they fall out of the same formula, so an over-moderated graphite core with boiling water gets a positive void coefficient by itself.

    Also modelled: I-135/Xe-135 and Pm-149/Sm-149 poisoning, burnup and conversion, four-group decay heat, fuel and moderator heat-up, boiling and DNB, natural circulation, coolant freezing, SG dry-out, condenser vacuum, ECCS, RHR and diesels.

    Things to try. Pick RBMK, switch off protection, and pull power down with rods while boiling — then scram (graphite-tipped rods add reactivity first). Put natural uranium in a light-water core and watch k∞ drop below 1. Push the moderator ratio right on a PWR until the coefficient turns positive. Run a station blackout and see how long the steam generators last. Load a sodium fast reactor and trip its pumps without a scram.

    Keys. Space pause · S scram · V switch view · F full width.

    Numbers are tuned to be the right size, not licensing-grade. It is a sandbox.
