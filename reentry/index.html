    Flying an entry

    A blunt capsule flies at a trimmed angle of attack, so it has a little lift (L/D ≈ 0.28). You can't pitch it — you roll the lift vector. Lift up stretches the trajectory (and can skip you back out); lift down shortens it and steepens the dive (more g, more heat). Rolling also turns you left or right, so real guidance flies a bank angle and reverses it to stay on line.

    Controls

    A / DRoll the lift vector left / right (20°/s)
    W · SRoll to full lift-up · full lift-down
    GPredictor-corrector guidance to the target
    JDeploy drogues, then mains (auto when armed)
    PShow the predicted splash point and reachable footprint
    C · dragCameras: chase, window, recovery ship, free

    Limits

    Crew≤ 8 g comfortable, > 12 g sustained is fatal
    Heat shieldpeak flux 6 MW/m², integrated load as listed
    Droguesbelow Mach 1.4 and 12 kPa
    Mainsbelow ~120 m/s
    Splashdown≤ 12 m/s

    Physics: 3-DOF point mass over a rotating Earth, US Standard Atmosphere 1976, convective heating by Sutton–Graves (k = 1.74×10⁻⁴, nose radius 6 m) and radiative heating by Tauber–Sutton above ~9 km/s, plasma blackout while the shock layer is ionised. The guidance is a numerical predictor-corrector: it integrates the rest of the flight several times a second to find the bank angle that lands on the target, with bank reversals for crossrange.
